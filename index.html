Featured Works

Cola Zero Adverstisement

A cinematic 3D product animation created in Cinema 4D as a class project, focusing on realistic materials, controlled lighting, and dynamic camera work. This piece explores high-end product visualization through precise modeling, shading, and render-driven storytelling inspired by commercial advertising.

Ferrari Window Display

A high-end 3D window display concept designed and modeled in Cinema 4D as a class project, inspired by Ferrari’s heritage of performance-driven elegance. The composition uses sculptural forms, controlled color contrast, and gallery-style lighting to translate automotive design principles—speed, precision, and motion—into a contemporary retail display. Emphasis was placed on material realism, spatial composition, and cinematic lighting to create a premium, exhibition-like atmosphere rather than a conventional storefront.

Editorial Magazine Cover

A fashion editorial cover created in Adobe Photoshop as a class project, blending contemporary photography with refined typography to evoke a high-end magazine aesthetic. The design emphasizes mood, negative space, and typographic contrast, with careful retouching and color grading to achieve a polished, runway-inspired visual narrative suitable for print.

Architectural Diorama

A 3D architectural diorama created in Cinema 4D as a class project, exploring the relationship between interior space and nature. The concept centers on a minimalist café environment defined by organic forms, natural materials, and soft, diffused lighting, with a skylight acting as the compositional focal point. The project emphasizes spatial balance, material subtlety, and atmosphere to evoke stillness, mindfulness, and a gallery-like sense of calm

REVIA - Skincare Brand Concept

A conceptual skincare brand developed as a class project, focused on simplicity, ritual, and nature-driven renewal. REVIA explores minimalist branding through restrained typography, a soft, earthy color palette, and sculptural packaging, translating the idea of skin restoration into a calm, elevated visual identity inspired by purity, balance, and modern luxury
